# Surface Brief: Lulu CRM Dashboard

## Scope
Comprehensive administrative CRM suite covering Overview, User Management & Profiles, Economy & Transaction Auditing, Item Catalog & Shop Manager, and Guild Bot Configuration.

## Mode
Operate

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A high-agency command bridge for the Lulu Discord Suite that replaces disconnected spreadsheets and slow Discord slash commands with a unified, high-density, real-time management console infused with Arcane Lulu magic.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep mystical obsidian and midnight indigo surfaces (#090714, #120e24, #1b1433) bordered by subtle violet luminescence (#2e2454). Accents are keyed to Lulu's iconic kit: Pix Gold (#f59e0b / #fbbf24) for high-value items/achievements, Whimsical Rose (#ec4899 / #f472b6) for Candies and Polymorphia states, and Arcane Violet (#8b5cf6 / #a855f7) for system operations. Form controls, tables, and badges feel tactile and magical with custom focus rings and tabular numerals.

### STORY
The administrator arrives to monitor server activity and immediately grasps economic health, active hexes, and top players. They search or inspect any player, modify candies or inventory with an automatic audit trail, toggle items in the catalog, or tune bot multipliers per guild with instant feedback.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A responsive command header with live guild selector and quick actions, followed by an asymmetric macro-metrics cluster (candies in circulation with daily velocity, active Polymorphia duels, unique players, and butterfly catches), flanked by a live Polymorphia active-hex alert ribbon and quick player search.

### FORM
Operate Command Dashboard with high-density data tables, drawer-ready user inspection, and modal-less inline administrative controls.
